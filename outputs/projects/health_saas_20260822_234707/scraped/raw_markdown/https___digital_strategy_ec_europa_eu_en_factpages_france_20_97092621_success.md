# France 2024 Digital Decade Country Report

France's performance towards the Digital Decade targets and objectives.

[Print as PDF](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/node/12845/printable/pdf)

![Logo of France's Digital Decade Report](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/sites/default/files/2024-06/2030DD%20Report%202024%20Title%20-%2016%20FR.jpg)

**In 2023, France made notable progress** in e-Health. However, **important challenges persist** in the digitalisation of SMEs. Although French enterprises need to progress in the take-up of advanced technologies (such as artificial intelligence and cloud), France has advanced in rolling out its fibre network.

## Two main strengths or areas of progress

### Connectivity infrastructure

France is on track to reach 100% coverage for gigabit, with 81.4% of households covered by Fibre to the Premises (FTTP) and an increase of 3.5 million fibre connections in 2023. 5G coverage stands at 93.2%, very close to the EU target and above the EU average of 89.3%. Moreover, 64.8% of French households covered by the 3.4-3.8 GHz band, an essential band for enabling advanced applications requiring large spectrum bandwidth.

### e-Health

In 2023, France improved its overall e-Health maturity, with access to e-health records scoring 79.3 out of 100, up from 54.5 in 2022 and in line with the EU average of 79.1.

## Two main weaknesses or areas to improve

### Digitalisation of SMEs

Although France hosts some innovative frontrunners, as a whole the global business sector underperforms on digitalisation. In 2023, 52% of SMEs had at least a basic level of digital intensity, below the EU average of 57.7%.

### Adoption of advanced digital technologies by enterprises

The take-up of cloud solutions by French enterprises (at 22.9% in 2023) is significantly below the EU average (38.9%). Similar trends can be seen in the adoption of ArtificiaI intelligence, standing at 5.9% in 2023 and below the EU average of 8.0%.

## France’s Digital Decade strategic roadmap

The French roadmap demonstrates **that the country plans** **some effort** to achieve the Digital Decade objectives and targets. It sets targets for for 12 out of 14 key performance indicators (KPIs) and, in most of the cases, targets are aligned with the EU 2030 ambitions. To achieve its digital transformation, France plans to allocate a total budget (excluding private investments) estimated at **EUR 10.2 billion (0.4% GDP).**

## Digital rights and principles

The Special Eurobarometer 'Digital Decade 2024' reveals some positive trends, such as the high importance that French people give to digital technologies for accessing public services (82%), indicating strong appreciation for digital advancements, and for connecting with friends and family (85%). However, concerns have risen, with only 39% of French respondents believing the EU protects their digital rights, 52% worried about the online safety for children, and 45% concerned about the control over personal data, reflecting growing unease. These findings underscore **the need to reinforce digital rights at national level.**
