## Why software as a service SaaS matters
The model is mature enough that growth-at-any-cost has given way to scrutiny of retention and gross margin, and buyers now consolidate overlapping tools rather than adding them. AI is the live question: it lowers the cost of building a competing product, while giving incumbents with proprietary data and workflow ownership a way to charge for outcomes instead of seats.
