# France

Meet our team of experts dedicated to helping restaurants optimize pricing, improve profitability, and tackle local challenges with data-driven, AI-powered solutions

Revenue Management Solutions supports the French restaurant industry with data-driven solutions. Our Paris-based team partners with operators to refine menu strategies, increase profitability, and navigate the unique challenges of the French market, helping restaurants thrive in one of Europe’s most competitive dining landscapes.

In addition to France, our associates also support restaurant brands in **Kuwait, South Africa, UAE** and **Saudi Arabia.**

Our AI-powered solutions support local and international brands across Europe. With decades of experience and a proven global track record, RMS enables restaurants to stay ahead in a fast-paced market.
